## Intended teacher to learner pipeline — not one prompt per fact
- LLM generates batches of synthetic *experiences*, dialogues, competing hypotheses, causal interventions, diverse role/clock interpretations. Tag as TEACHER_SIMULATED with full lineage, never OBSERVED_WORLD.
- Freeze holdouts *before* learning and use independent generators and simulator/physical host receipts when available. Similar/paraphrased messages from one teacher count as one origin, not many independent roots.
- C4 must create/modify its own general operators and graph relationships, not merely import teacher answers. Require heldout transfer, delayed consequences, revision under contradiction, and provenance-preserving forgetting/compression.
- A target size in MB or transformer parameters is not the training criterion. Measure unseen competence / retained abilities / semantic error propagation / memory and compute budget.
- After bootstrapping, let C4 propose questions and experiments, independently verify consequences, use LLM as optional bounded teacher/critic, not permanent ground truth.
- Danger: a teacher can hallucinate, rationalize or echo C4's error. Multiple dependent teacher samples are not independent evidence. G215 and cascade-safe reward/penalty are permanent admission gates.
