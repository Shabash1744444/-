# C4 FOUR-LAW CONSTITUTION

Status: normative architectural invariant.

## The four owners

1. **EVAL** — evaluation may generate hypotheses, comparisons, probabilities, interpretations and causal candidates, but an evaluation cannot make itself true.
   - shorthand: THOUGHT(X) != TRUTH(X)

2. **COMMIT** — only a supported basis with provenance may change recognized/canonical state.
   - history is not erased merely because current status changes;
   - a status change without a basis is illegal.

3. **DRIVE** — DRIVE is the sole arbiter of what the system does next.
   - goals, curiosity, emotions, tools, timers, external models and user messages may change MASK/VALUE/AVAIL/TRIGGER/STATUS;
   - none of them may directly seize action selection.

4. **MEDIATE** — internal intention/action request is not an external-world result.
   - an external effect requires boundary execution and a receipt/verified result before it may be recognized as having occurred.

## Five influence effects

- **MASK** — change admissibility.
- **VALUE** — change relative value/priority.
- **AVAIL** — change contextual availability.
- **TRIGGER** — request a new DRIVE arbitration cycle.
- **STATUS** — change recognized state/status through its lawful owner.

The coarse causal vocabulary is therefore:

4 source owners × 4 recipient owners × 5 influence types = **80 coarse interaction signatures**.

These are not 80 facts, 80 neurons or 80 hard-coded functions.

## Universal scope

The constitution applies to every semantic layer: dialogue, training, memory, perception, causal inference, self-model, other-model, tools, actions, simulation, multimodal binding and long-form narrative.

No chat protocol is a fifth law.

Consequences:
- ASK != obligation for the human to answer.
- NEXT_MESSAGE != ANSWER.
- UNKNOWN != FALSE.
- question status cannot be changed by a magic surface string such as `не знаю`.
- an open question cannot MASK DRIVE merely because it is open.
- several questions/interests may remain open while the process continues.
- a late answer may resolve an older question when EVAL identifies a supported semantic match and COMMIT changes its status.
- speech/action requests require DRIVE authorization and remain requests until MEDIATE obtains a boundary result.
- source claims may be committed as source claims without becoming external-world truth.

Any mechanism that cannot be expressed without violating these ownership rules is an architectural bug or an incomplete causal chain, not a reason to add a special-case cognitive law.
