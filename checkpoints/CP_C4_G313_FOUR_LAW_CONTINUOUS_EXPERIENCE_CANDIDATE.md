## Constitutional authority

No chat protocol is a cognitive law.

- **EVAL:** evaluation/hypothesis cannot self-declare truth.
- **COMMIT:** recognized/canonical status changes require a basis/provenance.
- **DRIVE:** sole arbiter of what the system does next.
- **MEDIATE:** intention/action request is not an external outcome; verified boundary result needs receipt.

Influence vocabulary only: MASK / VALUE / AVAIL / TRIGGER / STATUS.
4 × 4 × 5 = 80 coarse influence signatures, not 80 world facts.

## G313 removal of chatbot assumptions

- ASK is an event in the same experience line.
- NEXT_MESSAGE != ANSWER by temporal adjacency.
- An open question does not MASK DRIVE.
- Multiple questions may remain OPEN simultaneously.
- A late explicit semantic answer can close the matching older question.
- `не знаю` is not a magic control token and does not own question status.
- `pending_ask` is reduced to a short-lived deictic parsing aid, not the lifetime of a question.

A small sign may index a large meaning unit, but FORM != MEANING and PART != WHOLE.
