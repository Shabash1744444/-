## Constitutional test

Every semantic transition must answer two questions:

1. Which owner has authority here: EVAL, COMMIT, DRIVE, or MEDIATE?
2. Which typed influence occurs: MASK, VALUE, AVAIL, TRIGGER, or STATUS?

If the actual explanation is only "the parser recognized it", "the next message arrived",
"the teacher said it", "the question is open", "the test expects it", or
"this is convenient for chat", the transition is constitutionally suspect.

## Universal structural finding

There are multiple mutation doors into the durable graph.

1. EpistemicAdmissionOrgan -> guarded candidate/evidence path.
2. BootstrapTeacher -> direct graph.commit/replace.
3. C4ChildDialogue -> direct graph.commit/replace for USER_SAID and live teaching.

graph.truth() evaluates active committed facts as TRUE/FALSE without using origin or authority
to distinguish SOURCE_ASSERTION from WORLD_VERIFIED truth.

Therefore the same organism can correctly protect source independence in the epistemic organ
while a dialogue or corpus path bypasses that protection.

This is the largest constitutional defect found.

Required repair:
ALL canonical truth mutation must pass one COMMIT gate.
Source statements, corpus statements, narrative facts, user reports, observations,
simulation receipts and derived hypotheses must retain typed scope/status before they can
affect a requested truth domain.

USER_SAID(X) != WORLD_TRUE(X).
BOOK_SAYS(X) != WORLD_TRUE(X).
MODEL_SAYS(X) != WORLD_TRUE(X).
SIMULATION_RECEIPT(X) != PHYSICAL_WORLD_TRUE(X).

A source statement may itself be canonically recorded as a source statement.

## Constitutional migration rule for old weights

Do NOT simply delete historical learning.

For every existing direct fact, reconstruct from provenance:
- proposition content;
- source identity/lineage;
- origin;
- scope;
- evidence dependency;
- current status;
- whether it is observation, receipt, source assertion, narrative fact, language convention,
  simulator fact, SELF report, OTHER report, or derived hypothesis.

Then re-evaluate its authority for the requested truth domain.

The old fact may remain valid, but its status must be justified by COMMIT rather than by historical
presence in canonical storage.

## Priority repair order

P0 — one universal COMMIT gate in front of every graph truth mutation.
P1 — typed claim/scope layer so source statements are not automatically world truth.
P2 — DRIVE-only initiative: remove request/response and pending-question authority.
P3 — MEDIATE boundary shared by tools, body, sensors, simulator and external receipts.
P4 — continuous life-line and semantic composition.
P5 — replay/migrate old G222-G309 learning through the gate.
P6 — only then resume developmental training and live teaching.
