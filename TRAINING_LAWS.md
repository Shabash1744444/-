# C4 TRAINING LAWS

## Epistemic invariants
UNKNOWN != FALSE
REPLAY != NEW EVIDENCE
DERIVED != OBSERVATION
SIMULATION != OBSERVATION
PREDICTION != EVIDENCE
ACTION_REQUEST != VERIFIED_OUTCOME
RECEIPT != CAUSAL PROOF
SIMILARITY != IDENTITY
MODEL CONFIDENCE != AUTHORITY
CANONICAL != VERIFIED AUTHORITY
different source labels != independent lineage
one model / many chats != many independent sources
explanation != evidence

## Source/evidence independence
Independence requires both:
- independent source lineage;
- independent root/ancestry of the specific evidence.

A relay/copy does not become new evidence because it has a new name, channel, source family or model session.
Evidence dependency roots are externally attested and immutable; a claim may not mint its own trustworthy root.

## Error recovery
C4's accepted hypothesis must not become authority merely because it became canonical.
Correct independent refutation must be able to retract a false belief.
Retraction must remove unsupported consequences but preserve conclusions that remain supported through independent valid paths.

## Curriculum law
Do not train held-out answers directly.
Separate:
- directly taught primitive knowledge;
- novel positive transfer/inference;
- correct restraint/UNKNOWN;
- correction/recovery;
- persistence after restart.

Never report UNKNOWN controls as positive transfer.

## Repair law
Counterexample -> minimal general repair -> re-attack -> old regression -> cold reload -> physical checkpoint.
Do not hardcode the counterexample.
Do not add cognitive physics solely to make a benchmark green.

## Storage law
.c4m growth is not intelligence.
No byte padding.
Track runtime/code changes separately from organism growth.
