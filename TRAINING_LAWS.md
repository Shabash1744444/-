# C4 TRAINING LAWS

## Constitutional four laws

These four laws are global. They apply to chat, learning, memory, perception, initiative, reasoning, tools, embodiment and every semantic layer. No subsystem may create a local shortcut that bypasses them.

### 1. EVAL
Evaluation may propose, compare, score, interpret, predict, hypothesize or estimate, but it has no authority to make its own result true.

THOUGHT / HYPOTHESIS / INTERPRETATION / CONFIDENCE != TRUTH.

A result produced by EVAL remains a candidate until a lawful COMMIT basis exists.

### 2. COMMIT
Canonical state may change only from a lawful basis with provenance.

A new belief, correction, retraction, status change or current-state update requires admissible grounds. A later state does not erase the historical existence of the earlier state.

TEMPORAL ADJACENCY != COMMIT AUTHORITY.
NEXT UTTERANCE != ANSWER.
QUESTION CONTEXT != TEACHING AUTHORIZATION.

### 3. DRIVE
Only DRIVE arbitrates what C4 does next.

Goals, curiosity, emotion-like value, hypotheses, timers, user messages, questions, external models and tools may influence DRIVE, but none may seize action selection directly.

WANT / GAP / QUESTION / TRIGGER != ACTION DECISION.
OPEN QUESTION != DRIVE LOCK.

Chat request/response is not a cognitive law. A question is one event in the organism's continuous life-line. Several questions, pauses, delayed answers, recursive thought and later return are allowed when DRIVE selects them.

### 4. MEDIATE
Internal intention, request, utterance, prediction or tool command is not an external outcome.

Any effect that crosses the C4/world boundary requires separate execution and a world-side result/receipt.

ACTION_REQUEST != VERIFIED_OUTCOME.
PREDICTION != OBSERVATION.
UTTERANCE != WORLD CHANGE.

## Five lawful influence types

The four owners may causally affect each other only through typed effects. These are not additional laws.

- MASK — change admissibility: allowed / disallowed.
- VALUE — change value, priority, cost, urgency or desirability.
- AVAIL — change whether information, capability, object or action is available to a consumer in context.
- TRIGGER — signal that conditions changed and DRIVE should arbitrate again. TRIGGER does not choose the result.
- STATUS — change recognized state such as OPEN / BACKGROUND / RESOLVED / CONFIRMED / RETRACTED / HISTORICAL.

This gives the coarse interaction matrix:

4 sources × 4 recipients × 5 influence types = 80 causal classes.

These are classes of lawful influence, not 80 neurons, functions or hardcoded chat rules.

## Source/evidence independence
Independence requires both:
- independent source lineage;
- independent root/ancestry of the specific evidence.

A relay/copy does not become new evidence because it has a new name, channel, source family or model session.
Evidence dependency roots are externally attested and immutable; a claim may not mint its own trustworthy root.

## Error recovery
C4's accepted hypothesis must not become authority merely because it became canonical.
Correct independent refutation must be able to retract a false belief.
Retraction must remove unsupported consequences but preserve conclusions that remain supported through independent valid paths.

## Curriculum law
Do not train held-out answers directly.
Separate:
- directly taught primitive knowledge;
- novel positive transfer/inference;
- correct restraint/UNKNOWN;
- correction/recovery;
- persistence after restart.

Never report UNKNOWN controls as positive transfer.

## Repair law
Counterexample -> minimal general repair -> re-attack -> old regression -> cold reload -> physical checkpoint.
Do not hardcode the counterexample.
Do not add cognitive physics solely to make a benchmark green.

## Storage law
.c4m growth is not intelligence.
No byte padding.
Track runtime/code changes separately from organism growth.
