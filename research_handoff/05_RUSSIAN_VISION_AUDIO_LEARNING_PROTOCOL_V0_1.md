# C4: Research protocol LANG-VIS-AUD-0.1 — learn Russian through lived perception and action
Date: 2026-10-10. **NONCANONICAL RESEARCH PLAN — design proposal, NOT implemented and NOT measured.** C4 is separate from Singularity A7. Source: CL-R4_REPORT.md and the archived c4life S code (old b63927fc ZIP, not the final Claude 75d172b8 rebuild). External literature: BabyAI; BabyDS (Languages 2026); AAAI 2025 Learning Language Structures Through Grounding, and NeSyCoCo.

## Fundamental target, falsifiable
An agent S (or a documented competing architecture) must learn Russian as a system for grounding dialogue acts, roles, temporal assertions and causal events, not memorizing string-to-label mappings. It must correlate texts and human speech with its own first-person interactive visual/audio experience; propose, verify, revise and compose reusable language operations; generate natural new utterances. Independence of world verification and teacher source is non-negotiable. Prior Russian proficiency of LLM teacher does not automatically count as proficiency of S.

## Verified baseline boundary
CL-R4 S operator signatures over small finite domains 2 or 4; base primitives NOT AND OR XOR IF EQ GE ADD; enumerated finite functions, online prequential credit, Bayesian hypotheses, operator promotion in >=2 independent goals; lifetime journal and teacher channel. S transfers a learned operator across small invented causal device and entity worlds, 6/6 on reported six seeds; no direct Russian parser or native natural dialogue; old G331 DFP v3 parser separate and unmerged; hybrid H not implemented. NEED language sequence, variable length, open entities and compositional binding. P8 witness reconstruction after W replay red; S latent sibling possible; false certification noise persists; cross-code-upgrade missing. Do not run many-day irreversible training before robust migration/checkpoint.

## Engineering separation
World Engine: physics and deterministic scene/action verifier; provides authoritative receipts on own actions and actual state transitions within this virtual world. It NEVER gets its goals or outcomes solely from the LLM teacher; holdout worlds generated/reviewed independently.
MEDIATE: authenticates origin for raw camera pixels, raw audio/time sequence, world receipts, USER/TEACHER claims, manual feedback, ASR/TTS annotations. Captions and transcriptions by pretrained models are *teacher interpretations*, not native OBSERVATION. Keep original waveform/frame references and timestamp hash.
Representation: optional small trainable vision and audio frontends (CNN/SSM/vector quantizers and online embeddings) can produce uncertain candidate segments, tracks, acoustic units; MUST be explicitly trainable and independently evaluated; teacher foundation model used only for mentoring, not silent perpetual semantic backdoor. S currently cannot itself infer raw images/audio through 2/4-domain truth tables.
Cognitive learner: general programs over typed event frames, sequences, intervals, persons, variable references, actions, predicates, uncertainty, and source provenance. Candidate operations: SEGMENT, COMPARE, BIND, ALIGN, REFERENCE, COMPOSE, INFER/PLAN, GENERATE — these names are COMPUTATIONAL CAPABILITIES, NOT a Russian dictionary or fixed language rules. Learn compositions and if possible novel operation types. False/unknown separation remains.
Output: dialogue as selected DRIVE action, compositional language generator and speech synthesizer (voice from external TTS initially), not fixed canned replies. Observe and log outcomes of interaction.

## Curriculum, run controlled pilot BEFORE massive corpus
Gate 0. Fix/contain P8 missing witnesses, premature noisy certificates and CODE_UPGRADE; freeze baseline & cross-version replay. Retain fail-closed behavior. Generate heldout tasks before training.
Gate 1. One interactive room with two moving geometrical objects, independent color/shape/sound variants. Agent sees raw render or sealed frozen visual encoder outputs plus raw sensor references; actions pick/move/open/check and observables. Different shots must preserve object identity; world state is verifier only, NOT a magic percept oracle. Learn action effects and object permanence prior to labels.
Gate 2. Ground text forms using contrastive verified commands: «шар», «куб», «красный», «возьми», «положи», «рядом», «слева», «не трогай». Teacher gives contextual candidate examples, agent asks or intervenes, world returns actual outcome. Learn words by context/contrast; red new shape transfers adjective without explicit new pair; novel names deliberately held out. Explicit negatives.
Gate 3. Learn phrase structure and Russian inflections as induced surface alternations and their semantic roles, not manually assigned CASE tags as answers. Eg «возьми шар» vs «подойди к шару»; «куб на шаре» vs «шар на кубе»; «красный шар» vs «красная кнопка»; order reversal, number, negation, tense/aspect, conditional, multi-clause. Learn span -> typed event programs; train on short expressions, test unseen longer combinations, new words and unseen conjugations. No regex replacing induction.
Gate 4. Speaker/deixis/narratives: user says «я», agent says «я», third party says «я»; quoted speech, reported opinion, mistake/correction, relative story time vs physical clock, irony markers with optional uncertainty. Same string should have distinct referents depending on turn. Evaluate no world-fact promotion from fiction/quotes.
Gate 5. Audio concurrently with Gates 1–4: raw recorded/TTS Russian from many speakers, noises, tempo, prosody. Learn acoustic segmentation and alignment speech <-> meanings using matched and mismatched tasks; initially permit teacher-produced transcription as fallible hint, then remove it from test. Recognize sounds of environmental events (door, collision) also via temporal synchrony with vision and world receipts. Optional Whisper ASR for *baseline* or teacher, not as proof native C4 learned speech.
Gate 6. Image generalization concurrently with Gates 1–4: pixels -> object tracks/features with contrastive temporal invariances across viewpoints, color/texture/lighting, collisions and occlusions. If external pretrained DINOv2 or CLIP frozen encoder is used, report that perception was inherited and freeze/replace it in ablation. Test using new renderer and real camera snapshots separately. Do not feed authoritative object labels into C4 observation.
Gate 7. Conversational skills: question -> clarification -> corrected prediction -> action -> feedback; spontaneous report if significant, silence if no valuable news; truthful admission of unknown. Talk about past events, plans, feelings as attributions to speakers, uncertainty, not personally observed facts. Social language conventions confirmed by interaction and human feedback; not all discourse claims are falsifiable by physics.
Gate 8. Independent cross-world/language transfer: beyond devices/entities; code/debugging text, navigation, human social attribution, sound source localization. Train a general composition skill in A and test B without new solver or teacher leakage. Hold out operation type (count/variable arity), not only nouns and entity IDs.

## Exactly how LLM teacher works
- TEACHER proposes one lesson or a minimal contrast in structured envelope: raw Russian utterance, optional audio, candidate meaning as structured program, predicted measurable consequence, uncertainty, attribution; it cannot label its own lesson an OBSERVATION.
- C4 DRIVE chooses if/where to ask and whether to observe/test based on uncertainty, value, query token/GPU cost, world experimental cost and user burden. The earlier CL-R4 ~2–4 questions per 2000 ticks was tuned for expensive human; configurable teacher query price is required.
- A separate engine and withheld evaluator supplies independent outcome; evaluator is NOT the teacher. If the teacher and generated world share same error, independent confirmation fails; use code-defined world dynamics and separate adversarial human exam.
- A LLM audio transcription or image description is an ASSERTION attributed to a model, confidence attached, raw sensor remains separate. Different model 'teachers' may be correlated; count dependency, not labels.
- Positive lesson -> test new unseen contrast -> certificate only when independent evidence and source lineage intact. Negative -> log counterexample -> minimal repair -> reattack -> regression.
- For semantic conventions untestable through physical action (politeness, joke, irony), evaluate on witnessed speaker context, discourse consistency, preferences and rated interaction; keep subjective claims scoped.

## A first experiment: compositional Russian in one room
Training:
T1. 2 moving shapes; teacher says «Это шар» while grounded actor indicates the ball; C4 first observes teacher claim, not truth.
T2. Different color/shape; «Возьми красный шар», permission granted, C4 asks or acts; actual picked object receipt independently verified.
T3. «Положи шар слева от куба»; multiple layouts and novel positions; compare predicted outcome with actual.
T4. «Не бери красный шар»; a red decoy; quantify negation correctly.
T5. «Я вижу шар», spoken by speaker A; same utterance by B; semantic referent must swap. Self's own «я» refers to SELF, not user.
T6. «Учитель сказал: шар синий» with world visibly red; quote status ≠ sensed color.

Blind test includes new colored geometric object, changed camera, new speaker voice, unseen noun + known modifiers, reordered subject-object, time and negation, audio noise. Training phrase string NEVER identical to exact test phrase. Independent random seeds; tests registered before code tuning. Contradictory teacher deliberately injected.

## Candidate metrics (PROPOSED, not CL-R4 results)
For each stage: (1) grounded instruction execution and no unauthorized action; (2) compositional accuracy on heldout unseen speaker/item/perspective/scene/phrase; (3) precision+coverage of semantic answers and false-certification rate separately; (4) question cost and verified observations to criterion; (5) operator reuse across domains; (6) effect of ablations S vs S_NO_LIB vs W vs fixed DFP v3 vs S+learned sensor frontend; (7) source-lineage after full retraction/replay/cold restart/CODE_UPGRADE; (8) sample efficiency and CPU/GPU/RAM/latency. Hold out at least 100–200 independently authored cases per gate to start, expand and report confidence intervals. Suggested hard gate: >90% newly composed command success in constrained world, zero unlicensed actions or teacher-to-world claim laundering, and independently replicated seeds; these thresholds are RESEARCH targets, not current achievements.

## Strong cautions
A naive list «кошка = животное» builds taxonomy, not conversation. Text-only grammar exercises are insufficient for grounded meaning, but world grounding alone does not teach all language (fiction/irony/abstract reasoning must be learned via discourse and other evidence types). Pretrained ASR/VLM may be pragmatic scaffolding but hide what C4 truly acquired. No 'zero code priors': general learning mechanism, representation bias, sensory network, physics engine and action repertoire are engineering prior knowledge; experiment must isolate learned component. No promise of natural dialogue, sound understanding or world-model portability until blind tests.
